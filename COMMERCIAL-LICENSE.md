counts; this page says the same in plain words.

- **Testing, development, devnet, research and security audits** are free for everyone, with
  no notice.
- **Using the Vigilantis program that Softseco deploys** is free for everyone, whatever your
  size, with no notice: a mint whose transfer hook, freeze, pause or permanent-delegate

  program addresses are listed in the README once deployed.
- **Any other production use**, such as deploying your own copy of the program with your own
  upgrade authority, needs a commercial license from Softseco.
- Softseco sets the terms of every commercial license, and may grant one at no charge. A young
  company can, for example, get a license that is free until it grows past an agreed size.

Sentinel, the earlier name of this program, stays available under Apache-2.0 up to 2.3.0 at
github.com/softseco/sentinel.

## Asking for a commercial license

Write to **developers@softseco.com** with your company's name, what you are building, the
network and mints you plan to run Vigilantis for, and whether you need your own upgrade
authority. We reply to every request.

## What we do with what you send

Softseco (Joachim Szamborski) keeps your request to answer it and to manage your license. We do
not share it or use it for marketing. We keep it while your license lasts and for three years
after your last contact with us, unless the law requires longer. You can ask to see, correct or
delete it at developers@softseco.com.
